# Stage 1: Discovery (`1_discovery`)

## Project Objective
Develop a personal developer portfolio designed to showcase 9+ years of senior web development experience while explicitly positioning for a transition into AI Engineering and agentic workflows. Software development and AI automation are framed here as business accelerators, never technical bottlenecks.

## Design & UX Identity
- **Aesthetic:** 8-bit/retro styling driven by `nes.css`.
- **Color Palette:** Tokyo Night theme.
- **Responsiveness:** Mobile-first, fully responsive website design that scales the 8-bit elements cleanly across all viewports without breaking layout grids.

## Methodology
- Strict adherence to the Interpretable Context Methodology (ICM) to ensure Antigravity IDE agents have a deterministic understanding of the project state and architecture.
