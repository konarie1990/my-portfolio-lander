Why 29 ➔ 60+ Matters More Than 60 ➔ 90

Overcoming user abandonment creates the revenue hockey-stick; chasing 90+ yields diminishing returns.

Lighthouse vs. Real Conversion Rate Curve

The Abandonment Cliff

*Going from 29 to 60+ removes the barrier for ready-to-buy shoppers. Moving 65 to 95 rarely shifts revenue.

The "Abandonment Cliff" (Scores 20–35)

A score of 29 on mobile means LCP takes 4.5 to 7+ seconds and INP lags 400ms–1,000ms+. On paid Meta/TikTok/Google Shopping traffic, shoppers tap variant swatches or "Add to Cart" and assume the page is frozen, or bounce before the buy box ever hydrates.

The 60+ Friction-Removal Zone

Jumping from a broken, main-thread-blocked 29 to a responsive 60+ unblocks the 10% to 15% of high-intent shoppers who were actively trying to buy but bailed due to slow scripts.

Diminishing Returns (65 ➔ 95)

Once LCP is under 2.5s and INP is under 200ms, human perception limits are satisfied. Trimming an extra 80ms rarely shifts purchase behavior. Target the 60–75 green zone where revenue is won.

03

The "Synthetic Score" Trap

Lighthouse is a weighted formula. Not all points convert equally.

💰

Points That Print Money

Directly removes user-facing friction on the mobile Product Display Page (PDP) & Cart.

1. Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) Under 2.0s +CVR Driver

Hero image, price, title, and Add-to-Cart appear instantly before mobile users swipe back to social feeds.

2. Total Blocking Time (TBT) Unblocked No Frozen Swatches

Clearing heavy JS execution so when shoppers tap variant swatches, it reacts immediately with zero lag.

3. Interaction to Next Paint (INP) Under 150ms Instant Cart Drawer

Eliminates the "double-tap rage click" where users tap ATC repeatedly because the cart drawer takes 800ms to open.

⚠️

Points That Do Nothing (Paper Score)

Improves the artificial score on paper from 29 to 50, resulting in 0% conversion lift.

1. Deferring Off-Screen Footer Analytics 0% Revenue Lift

Moving scripts from 4,000px down the page adds 8 Lighthouse points but does nothing for the top-of-funnel mobile experience.

2. Minor Color Contrast Adjustments Synthetic Only

Good for accessibility standards, but won't prevent an impatient TikTok shopper from bouncing on slow load.

3. Lazy-Loading Above-The-Fold Hero Image Counter-Productive!

Tricks naive audits by delaying image requests, but actively wrecks real user LCP and spikes bounce rates.
